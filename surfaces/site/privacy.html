Privacy Policy

CoopCupid is a Chrome extension that ranks WaterlooWorks jobs against your résumé, saves jobs to a WaterlooWorks folder, and can submit applications for you. It is built to keep your data on your own device. This policy explains what it handles and where that data goes.

The short version

  • Your résumé and matching data are stored only in your browser.
  • CoopCupid talks only to WaterlooWorks, using your own signed-in session.
  • There are no CoopCupid servers, accounts, analytics, ads, or AI services.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

What CoopCupid stores on your device

Stored in the extension’s local browser storage (IndexedDB):

  • Your résumé: the PDF you choose, its file name, and the text extracted from it, including any corrections you make.
  • Your preferences: completed co-op terms, the interview score cutoff, and whether automatic saving is on.
  • Search results: job postings read from your last WaterlooWorks search (titles, employers, locations, descriptions, application requirements) and their scores.
  • Application records: the outcome of your last application batch, and a temporary marker for a submission that was interrupted, so CoopCupid can warn you to check it.

Résumé parsing and job ranking happen entirely on your device.

What is sent, and to whom

CoopCupid sends data only to WaterlooWorks (waterlooworks.uwaterloo.ca), from your browser, in the session you are already signed into:

  • Searching reads job postings and your folder list from WaterlooWorks.
  • Saving creates a coopcupid folder and adds or removes jobs in it.
  • Applying uploads your résumé PDF to WaterlooWorks and submits applications you confirmed. Once uploaded, those documents and applications are held by WaterlooWorks under the University of Waterloo’s own policies.

CoopCupid never sends your data to its developer or any other third party. It never reads or stores your WaterlooWorks password.

Permissions

  • Access to waterlooworks.uwaterloo.ca: to read your search and act on your behalf on that site only. CoopCupid does not run on any other website.
  • activeTab and scripting: to work with the WaterlooWorks tab you have open.
  • sidePanel: to show CoopCupid beside the page.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays in your browser until you remove it. Clear local data in the side panel deletes everything CoopCupid stored. Removing the extension from Chrome deletes it too. Documents and applications already sent to WaterlooWorks stay in WaterlooWorks; manage them there.

This website

The CoopCupid website uses no cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions or concerns: open an issue on GitHub.
